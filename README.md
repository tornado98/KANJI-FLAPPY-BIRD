# 🐦 Kanji Flappy Bird

A classic Flappy Bird-style arcade game with an educational twist! Test your reflexes while learning and practicing Japanese Kanji characters.

## 🚀 Features

- **Gamified Learning:** Practice Japanese Kanji meanings or characters as you play.
- **Classic Arcade Mechanics:** Smooth physics and addictive gameplay inspired by the original Flappy Bird.
- **Lightweight & Fast:** Built for the web.

## 🎮 How to Play

1. Click or press Space to keep your character flying.
2. Navigate through the obstacles while recognizing the correct Kanji.
3. Beat your high score!
